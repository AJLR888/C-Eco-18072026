Problem

Business IT is often managed across disconnected systems, suppliers, and spreadsheets.

  • Limited visibility over which devices exist and where they are located.
  • Equipment replaced before the end of its useful life.
  • Residual value lost during storage, disposal, or replacement.
  • Repair, resale, recycling, and data-destruction records stored separately.
  • Difficulty comparing financial, operational, and environmental options.

Solution

A clearer lifecycle for every workplace device.

C-Eco is developing a system that brings device records, lifecycle decisions, service providers, and supporting evidence into one clearer process.

Track

Create a reliable record of devices, ownership, location, age, and condition.

Assess

Evaluate useful life, residual value, repair potential, risk, and environmental impact.

Decide

Identify whether equipment should be retained, repaired, redeployed, resold, or recycled.

Verify

Retain evidence relating to resale, recycling, data destruction, and lifecycle outcomes.

How it works

How the future C-Eco process will work

1

Record the organisation's IT devices.

2

Assess condition, value, useful life, and risk.

3

Compare reuse, repair, resale, and recycling options.

4

Connect the organisation with appropriate service providers.

5

Store evidence and report lifecycle outcomes.

C-Eco is currently validating this process with potential customers and industry partners.

Benefits

Better decisions across the complete IT lifecycle.

Control

Improve visibility over devices, locations, ownership, condition, and lifecycle status.

Cost

Extend useful life, reduce unnecessary replacement, and recover residual value.

Compliance

Maintain clearer records relating to disposal, recycling, and data destruction.

Sustainability

Support reuse, refurbishment, resale, and responsible recycling decisions.

Audience
